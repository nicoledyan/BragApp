Restore a purchase

In Brag, go to Settings → Plan → Restore purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the Apple ID used to subscribe.

◷

Manage your subscription

Apple handles billing. In Brag, go to Settings → Plan → Manage subscription, or open Apple Subscriptions.

$

Request a refund

Purchases are processed by Apple. Submit refund requests directly at reportaproblem.apple.com.

×

Delete your account

In Brag, go to Settings → Danger Zone → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all your wins.

✓

A quick fix that often helps

Close and reopen Brag, confirm you’re online, and check the App Store for an update. If the issue continues, email the iPhone or iPad model, iOS version, and what you expected to happen.
